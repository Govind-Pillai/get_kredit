What GetKredit Does for Your Business

One source of truth for your entire project

No more version confusion between your sales MIS and your admin records. GetKredit is a single, structured repository for every project — towers, units, prices, RERA docs, payment plans, construction status, and marketing materials — all updated in real time, all role-controlled.

Know exactly where every lead stands

Your Sales Managers and Executives log every interaction — meetings, site visits, follow-up calls, notes — against each lead. You can see which units are on hold at negotiation, which bookings are awaiting approval, and which leads are going cold. No guesswork.

Get your projects APF-approved faster

Your Credit team works directly with banks on the GetKredit platform — selecting the right FIs for your project, coordinating due diligence, managing document queries, and tracking APF validity and renewal timelines. Everything is documented. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Turn more sold customers into home loan clients

Your customers are your best home loan leads. GetKredit's Calling Process module ensures every booked customer is systematically called, profiled, matched to the right bank, and guided through the loan process — with full TAT tracking and performance analysis.

MIS that your leadership team will actually read

Real-time dashboards for leads, unsold inventory, sales target achievement, broker activity, and home loan conversion rates — for every project, every team, every role. Know your numbers without pulling a report.

Approval workflows that protect your business

Every booking, cancellation, lead transfer, and unit change goes through a structured approval flow with mandatory remarks. Nothing is confirmed without the right person signing off. Full audit trail, always.

Broker management that actually drives referrals

Onboard your broker network — companies, individuals, and sub-brokers — on GetKredit. Track every lead they send, every commission earned, and every target met. Give your Broker Admins the tools to run their teams, and your individual brokers visibility into their own pipeline.
