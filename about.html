About GetKredit

Real Estate in India moves fast. Projects get sold before they're built. Home Loans close in the last week of the quarter. RERA compliance is non-negotiable. And somehow, most of this is still managed on Excel and WhatsApp — leaving developers, banks, and home buyers struggling with fragmented information.

We built GetKredit to change that.

GetKredit is India's first unified Real Estate Ecosystem platform — purpose-built For Developers, banks & financial institutions, and home buyers. Whether you're managing a Project Portfolio, processing pre-qualified Home Loan applications, or searching for your dream home with the right financing, GetKredit brings every stakeholder together in one structured, role-controlled system.

Our platform is designed around real workflows: the ones that actually exist in developer organisations, bank Credit Teams, and home buyer journeys across India. Not generic CRM flows. The real ones that drive India's property market.
