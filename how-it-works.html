Lead to Booking

Who does this: Sales Executives & Sales Managers

2.1 — Add a Lead

Any authorised user adds a lead with full customer details — name, contact, DOB, city, source (broker, website, advertisement, etc.), and lead category (Hot / Warm / Cold). The lead is assigned to a Sales Executive immediately.

2.2 — Progress the Lead

Each lead moves through a defined lifecycle with mandatory remarks at every stage. The full activity timeline is visible to the Sales Manager and Admin.

2.3 — Add Co-Owners

Sales Executives add co-owners with KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving Licence, or Passport), employment proof, and income proof — directly within the lead record.

2.4 — Approval

Booking requests, unit changes, cancellations, and lead transfers go through GetKredit's structured approval flow. Your Admin reviews, adds a mandatory remark, and approves or rejects. The Sales Executive is notified immediately.

APF & Home Loan

Who does this: GetKredit Credit Team (with your Admin and Sales team)

APF (Approved Project Finance)

1

Bank / FI Selection

GetKredit's Credit team selects the right banks from their empanelled database — based on geography, portfolio, customer preference, and project fit.

2

Application & Due Diligence

Formal proposal submitted. Legal DD, Technical DD, and Regulatory DD (RERA) coordinated through the platform.

3

Query Management

Banks, DD agencies, GetKredit team, and your Admin can raise and respond to queries — all in one place. Nothing gets lost in email.

4

APF Issuance

Bank issues APF approval letter. Unique APF code assigned. Approved units, pricing, and agreed payment plan mapped. Home loan processing enabled.

5

Post-APF

APF validity tracked. Renewal alerts set. Conditions shared with all stakeholders.

How Follow-Up Logic Works

GetKredit reads the Additional Remark and auto-categorises intent:

High Intent
Next call within 12 business hours
Current Concern
Targeted follow-up addressing the specific concern
Future Potential
Milestone-based follow-up (next demand, construction update)
Low Priority
Weekly tracking
Auto Actions

If Loan Process Initiated = Yes, customer removed from calling queue automatically. If Senior Intervention Required = Yes, Sales Manager notified immediately for same-day escalation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a project live on GetKredit?

Most projects are configured and live within a single working session — typically under 2 hours for a standard residential project.

Does my sales team need training?

The platform is designed to be intuitive. Most Sales Executives and Managers are operational within their first session. Our team runs an onboarding call to get everyone aligned.

Can I manage multiple projects on GetKredit?

Yes. GetKredit is built for multi-project developer groups. Each project is scoped independently, with its own team, inventory, and data — but you get portfolio-level visibility through MIS.

Is GetKredit RERA compliant?

RERA compliance is built into the platform — RERA numbers, certificates, escrow bank accounts, revised RERA support, and document management are all core features.

What about my broker network?

GetKredit includes full broker management — onboard companies, individuals, and sub-brokers; track leads sourced by each broker; manage brokerage and incentive payouts.

How does APF work on the platform?

GetKredit's Credit team manages the APF process end-to-end alongside your Admin team — bank selection, due diligence, query management, APF issuance, and renewal tracking — entirely within the platform.

Is my data secure?

Yes. OTP-based login, role-based access control, and secure encrypted storage for all KYC documents and financial data.
