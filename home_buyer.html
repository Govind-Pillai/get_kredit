What Home Buyers Actually Face

You Don't Know Which Bank is Right

Most buyers have no idea which bank fits their profile — their income type, CIBIL score, employment status, or the specific project they've booked. So they apply blindly. Wrong bank. Wrong eligibility. Wasted time.

Multiple Applications Damage Your CIBIL

Every application triggers a hard inquiry on your CIBIL report. Apply to five banks that were never right for you, and you've just hurt your credit score — making it harder to get approved at the one bank that actually fits.

Non-Standard Profiles Get Rejected Outright

Self-employed? Irregular income? CIBIL score slightly below 750? Co-applicant needed? Most banks will decline you immediately — without understanding your actual financial situation or trying to structure the right solution.

You're Chasing the Bank, Not the Other Way Around

Submit documents. Wait. Follow up. Submit more documents. Wait again. No visibility into where your application stands. No one to negotiate on your behalf. You're just another file in the queue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does applying through GetKredit cost me anything?

No. GetKredit's home loan assistance service is free for home buyers. We earn from the banks we partner with — not from you. You pay nothing upfront, nothing during the process, and nothing at disbursement.

How long does the process take?

From initial call to sanction letter, typically 7–14 working days for straightforward profiles. Complex profiles or additional document requirements may take longer. GetKredit's job is to keep things moving — we follow up daily so you don't have to.

What if my CIBIL score is low?

A low CIBIL score doesn't automatically disqualify you. GetKredit assesses your overall profile — income stability, existing relationships, co-applicant strength, and the specific lender's criteria. We work to find solutions even for imperfect credit histories. Sometimes a co-applicant or additional collateral can help. We'll be honest about what's possible.

Which banks does GetKredit work with?

GetKredit is empaneled with leading public sector banks, private banks, and housing finance companies across India. The specific banks available to you depend on your profile and the project you've booked. We maintain relationships with multiple lenders so we can match you to the right one — not just the nearest branch.

What documents will I need?

Standard requirements include: identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, income proof (salary slips or ITRs), bank statements, employment/business proof, and property documents. GetKredit provides a structured checklist upfront — you'll know exactly what's needed before you start. Nothing is asked twice.

Can NRI buyers use GetKredit?

Yes. GetKredit works with NRI buyers purchasing property in India. The process involves additional documentation (passport, visa, overseas employment proof, NRE/NRO statements), and we coordinate with banks that have specific NRI home loan products. Distance is not a barrier — we manage everything remotely until disbursement.
