About GetKredit

Real estate in India moves fast. Projects get sold before they're built. Home loans close in the last week of the quarter. RERA compliance is non-negotiable. And somehow, most of this is still managed on Excel and WhatsApp.

We built GetKredit to change that.

GetKredit is a comprehensive real estate and credit management platform — purpose-built for developer organisations, their sales teams, their broker networks, and their home loan operations. We connect every stakeholder in the property sales lifecycle — from the builder's admin team to the bank's credit officer — in one structured, role-controlled system.

Our platform is designed around real workflows: the ones that actually exist in builder organisations, credit teams, and brokerage firms across India. Not textbook CRM flows. The real ones.

What GetKredit Covers

GetKredit is not a point solution. It is a full-lifecycle platform.

Project & Inventory

From RERA registration to unit configuration to construction updates.

Sales Pipeline

Every lead, every interaction, every booking — end to end.

Customer Management

Home Loan Processing

What We Believe

Real estate is relationships and data

We built GetKredit to capture both. Every interaction is logged. Every document is stored. Every decision has a trail.

Structure drives conversion

The teams that convert the most leads to home loans are the ones with the most disciplined processes — not the biggest teams.

Compliance isn't optional

RERA documentation, DSA agreements, APF covenants — made part of your daily workflow, not a Friday scramble.

Visibility is leadership

Your Sales Managers can only lead when they know what's happening. Your leadership can only decide when they have real data.

Built with builders, for builders

GetKredit is built in close collaboration with developer groups, credit teams, and brokerage firms. Every module reflects how these organisations actually work — and evolves as their needs change.
