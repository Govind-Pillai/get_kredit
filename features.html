Calling Process & Performance

Structured calling. Zero missed conversions.

Assigned calling dates

Every customer assigned to a specific Sales Manager with an auto-set calling date.

Call logging

Call Status (Picked / Not Picked), Call Remark, Reason (dropdown), and Additional Remark (mandatory decision-driving field).

Follow-up logic

System categorises intent (High / Medium / Low) and auto-assigns next calling date based on the Additional Remark.

TAT tracking

Turnaround Time measured per call; delayed calls tracked separately. Escalation triggers same-day notification.

Performance analysis

Total Calls Assigned vs Done vs Remaining; Effective vs Non-Effective; On-Time vs Delayed; root cause analysis for every non-effective call.

What Gets Measured

Total Calls Assigned vs Done Is your SM making calls?
Effective vs Non-Effective Are calls driving decisions?
On-Time vs Delayed Is the team on schedule?
Recovery Conversions Not Interested → Interested
File Logins Loan processes initiated
Auto Follow-Up Logic

High Intent → 12 hours. Current Concern → targeted follow-up. Future Potential → milestone-based. Low Priority → weekly tracking.
