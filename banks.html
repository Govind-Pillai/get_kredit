What GetKredit Delivers to Your Credit Team

No speculative leads. No incomplete files. No chasing developers for missing documents.

Profile-Matched Applications Only

We do not blast your inbox with every inquiry. Your eligibility criteria — CIBIL range, income type, LTV ratios, preferred asset class — are mapped in our database. Only customers who match your profile receive your application.

This means higher conversion rates, faster processing, and fewer declined applications wasting your credit team's time.

Complete Documentation Upfront

By the time an application reaches your desk, GetKredit has already collected and verified: CIBIL report, income proof, employment verification, identity documents, property booking details, and co-applicant KYC.

You receive a structured dossier — not a half-filled form with a phone number.

Pre-Verified Project Due Diligence

Every project we bring for APF has already cleared our DD coordination — legal title verification, technical approvals, RERA registration, and regulatory compliance. Your team reviews our documentation; you do not start from zero.

Raise queries through the platform. We coordinate resolution with the developer directly.

Approved Project Finance

APF Means Access to Every Buyer of That Project

Once your bank grants APF for a developer's project, every buyer of that project becomes a potential home loan customer for your institution. GetKredit manages the pipeline.

Due Diligence, Coordinated

Legal, technical, and regulatory verification managed by GetKredit's Credit team. You review structured documentation; we handle developer coordination.

Structured Query Management

Raise questions on APF documents or insurance submissions directly through the platform. No email chains. No lost follow-ups. Clear audit trail for compliance.

APF Renewal Tracking

We track validity periods and coordinate renewals. Your approved projects stay current without manual monitoring.

GetKredit maintains a structured, internal database of empanelled banks — interest rates, LTV ratios, product types, CIBIL ranges, and preferred asset classes. This database is used exclusively by GetKredit's Credit team to match the right bank to each customer and project.
